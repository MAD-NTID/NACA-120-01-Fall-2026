Problem 1 – Game Shopping

Imagine your character is going on a quest to acquire game-related items
from a virtual marketplace.

Using top-down design, break down the process of acquiring these items
into smaller tasks.

Start with the overall goal and identify the major tasks needed to
complete it. Then, break each major task into smaller subtasks.

Continue breaking down the tasks until each step is simple and easy
to understand.

Your solution should clearly show the different levels of the
top-down design.


1. Determine What Items Are needed (big task)
    1.1 Review character's current inventory and attributes
    1.2 Identify items needed for upgrading character
    1.3 Determine available budget (do I have enugh money?)

2. Search the Virtual Marketplace (big task)
    2.1 Open the marketplace (or go to game marketplace)
    2.2 Select an item category
    2.3 Compare available items

3. Select Items (big task)
    3.1 Check item price (smaller task, affordable?)
    3.2 Check attributes (better or worst)
    3.3 Does it fit the character? (role based equipment)
    3.4 Add it to the cart

4. Purchase Item(s) (big task)
    4.1 Go to shopping cart
    4.2 Find possible discounts (if applicable)
    4.3 Verify the total cost
    4.4 Check total (have enough mola/money/dinero/cashing/cash)
    4.5 Proceed to pay (buy)

5. Verify Purchase (big task)
    5.1 Review confirmation of purchase
    5.2 Open character's inventory to check that item has been added
