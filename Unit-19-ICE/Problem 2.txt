Problem 2 – Ordering a Pizza

Use top-down design to break down the process of ordering a pizza online.

Start with the overall goal:

    Order a Pizza Online

Identify the major tasks required to complete the process, such as:

- Selecting a pizza
- Choosing toppings
- Providing a delivery address
- Making payment

Break each major task into smaller subtasks.

Continue breaking down the tasks until each step is simple and easy
to understand.

Your solution should clearly show the different levels of the
top-down design.


1. Selecting a Pizza - Determine ordering type of pizza (menu selection) (big task)
    1.1 Type of pizza available
    1.2 Select pizza category
    1.3 Compare prices of pizzas
    1.4 Select pizza and size and crust
    1.5 Select sauce, type of cheese, main ingredients

2. Choose toppings
    2.1 Type of toppings available
    2.2 Select pizza toppings (extra toppings if needed)
    2.3 Remove unwanted toppings
    2.4 Compare prices of toppings (extras)
    2.5 Confirm pizza selection

3. Pick up or Delivery?
    3a. Pick up
        3a.1 - Select nearest store
        3a.2 - Estimate time for pick up
        3a.3 - Pick up Person Name and maybe phone # or order #
        3a.4 - Tip amount (optional)

    3b. Delivery
        3b.1 - Delivery cost (are you broke?)
        3b.2 - Select nearest store
        3b.3 - Estimate delivery 
        3b.4 - Tip amount (optional)
        3b.5 - Enter customer address
        3b.6 - Delivery instructions

4. Make Payment
    4.1 Review order and total cost
    4.2 Select payment method (credit card, debit, gift card, etc)
    4.3 Enter payment information
    4.4 Submit payment

5. Confirm Order
    5.1 Check email/text for receipt
    5.2 Verify confirmation 
    5.3 Check order status (estimated time/delivery)
